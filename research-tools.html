PAPER TOOL / BETTING CALCULATOR

Check the return before you record it.

Enter the actual decimal quote and your total paper stake. This calculator shows possible returns; it does not recommend a bet or place one.

Return calculatorDecimal odds · Australian dollars

If the horse wins, the gross return includes your stake.

Break-even chance

For a single decimal quote, divide 1 by the odds. That is the success rate needed to break even before any other costs. It is not the horse’s true chance.

Same-race multis

Use the bookmaker’s actual combined quote. Outcomes in the same race depend on each other, so multiplying leg odds gives a misleading price.

Record a forward test

07 / METHOD & DATA

How this workspace works.

The personal workspace runs in your browser. If the optional protected feed is configured, its PuntersEdge key remains on the server; no bookmaker account is connected. The private feed is for the site owner’s personal use under the provider’s Free-plan licence; do not share the access code or its prices with other people.

01

Enter the card

Add a race and its runners manually, or request the protected feed. Save the reported ground and observation time. A stored quote is a research snapshot, never a guaranteed current offer.

02

Freeze a paper selection

Before the listed jump time, record a win/place choice or a two-runner Top 3/Top 4 combination. The combined multi quote must be entered from the bookmaker; the app never multiplies leg prices.

03

Enter final results

After the race, load or enter official positions and mark the result final. Straightforward outcomes settle automatically. Use the special-outcome control for dead heats, deductions or unclear terms.

04

Read the limits

Everything is stored in this browser on this device. Clearing browser data loses it. Export a backup below. These tools do not backtest historical odds or validate a fair winning chance. Any market-consensus estimate is an unproven research signal.
